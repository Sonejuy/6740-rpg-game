How to play

You start at (1, 1, 1, 1, 1) and have — points to distribute across the five attributes — add or remove them anytime with the +/− controls above. Your raw attribute vector runs through the fixed synergy matrix once per point spent, then gets rescaled by your whole build's overall size — so the effective element power above rewards the shape of your build (which attributes you favor, relative to each other) more than just how many points you've spent. Try to maximize total damage output.

Synergy between attributes (symmetric — applies both ways)

Hint: the synergy is further realized through the mechanic Gt(x) = ‖Btx‖2‖x‖2 — each attribute's effective power comes from the raw synergy transform, rescaled by your whole build's overall size (its length as a vector). That rescaling is exactly why a lopsided, single-attribute build no longer wins — only a build shaped like the matrix's own strongest growth direction gets close to the theoretical max.
